## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use Sleeppy, you may provide:

- Account information, such as your display name, email address, and sign-in provider details, when you use available sign-in methods.
- Child profile information, such as names, dates of birth, photos, theme selections, and other details you choose to enter.
- Caregiving and activity records, such as sleep, feeding, diaper, child metrics, timers, notes, and related history.
- Family workspace information, such as invitations, membership actions, and shared family-scoped records.
- AI Assistant content, including prompts you send, chat history, selected child context, and any optional child notes you choose to include.
- Support communications and any information you include when you contact us.

### B. Information Collected Automatically

We also collect or generate:

- Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version, app build, language, time zone, and diagnostic metadata.
- Crash and diagnostic data through Firebase Crashlytics.
- Pseudonymous analytics data through Amplitude, including a generated app-specific analytics identifier, usage events, and app-level properties.
- Subscription and entitlement metadata from Apple and StoreKit, such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase state, and expiration timing.
- Push notification tokens and delivery-related metadata when notifications are enabled.
- Security and configuration data used by Firebase services, such as App Check, Remote Config, and backend integrity controls.

### C. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Much of Sleeppy's functionality uses on-device storage first. This can include child profiles, activity history, settings, and cached AI chat data.

Local app data may also be included in Apple device backups if your device backup settings allow that. Local-only storage does not mean the data can never appear in an iCloud or computer backup created by Apple for your device.

### D. Information Synced to Our Backend

If you sign in, Sleeppy can sync selected data to Firebase-backed services so it can be restored on your account and, where applicable, shared within your family workspace.

This may include:

- user profile and account settings,
- child profiles,
- caregiving logs and related metadata,
- family membership and invite records,
- AI Assistant consent state and usage counters,
- account-scoped AI threads and messages,
- notification token records,
- premium-state snapshot metadata.

## 4. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only as described below.

### A. Service Providers and Infrastructure

We use service providers that process data for us or on our behalf, including:

- **Apple** for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, and platform services.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep information for different periods depending on the data type and why it is needed.

- On-device data remains on the device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or clear app data, subject to any Apple-managed backups.
- Cloud-synced account and family data is retained until you delete it, clear it through supported product controls, or delete your account, unless we need to keep limited records for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or backup recovery.
- AI chats are retained until you delete them through the app, delete your account, or we need short-term retention for abuse prevention, diagnostics, or backup recovery.
- Push notification tokens are retained until they are refreshed, removed, invalidated, or no longer needed.
- Crash and analytics records are retained according to the applicable provider retention settings and policies.

Backups, logs, and security copies may remain for a limited additional period before they are overwritten or deleted.
